Four Walls social artwork

The two decorative PNGs were generated with the built-in image_gen tool,
then restyled against the website's own images/assets/ils_01.fw.svg illustration
(rasterized only as a style reference). They are generic decorative drawings,
not depictions of a listed property, its neighbourhood, or its condition.
Property photography is supplied separately, unchanged, from EstatePrime.

Final files: illustration-street.png and illustration-door.png.
Format: 1024 x 1536 PNG with alpha transparency.

Final prompt shared by both assets:
Restyle and replace the decorative artwork in image1 using image2 as the EXACT
brand style reference, not as a subject to reproduce wholesale. Image2 is the
existing Four Walls website illustration. Match the website's flat naive
architectural geometry, thin navy #1C3457 pencil outlines, tactile graphite
crosshatching and scribbled shading, playful proportions, sparse hot pink
#FF0062 accents, white/transparent open interiors. A few lines may be naturally
doubled or imperfect like pencil on paper. Modern, funky, minimalist, high-end
editorial graphic. Absolutely remove the old traditional watercolor style:
no realistic stone, no ornate balconies, no flowering bougainvillea, no detailed
foliage, no beige wash, no photorealistic perspective, no heavy old-fashioned
doorway. Palette only navy, graphite grey, white and hot pink. Art must be light
and airy, not dense or dark. No type, logos, labels, numbers, borders, watermark
or paper-background rectangle. This is a reusable decorative cutout for a
social-media template. Output tall portrait 2:3 with genuine alpha transparency,
generous clear space.

Street subject:
A quirky cluster of three simple geometric houses and one skinny apartment
building, playful slightly skewed roofs, irregular rectangular windows, a pink
front door, a round pink attic window, a tiny lollipop tree, sparse scribbly
ground strokes. It should feel like a fun architect's pencil doodle,
intentionally simple, open and clever. Buildings sit mostly on the right and
bottom; keep the left half transparent for compositing a photo.

Door subject:
One playfully skewed simple open doorway and an oversized pink key floating near
it, two loose pencil steps, one tiny abstract plant made of a few curved pencil
strokes. The door is a clean rectangular geometric shape with sparse
crosshatched pencil shading, a single bold pink plane on its inside edge, and a
small circular handle. It should feel like a fun architect's pencil doodle,
intentionally simple, open and clever. Keep transparent space around the drawing.

Typography:
Noto Serif Display SemiBold is a static instance of the upright Google Fonts
variable font, wght=600 and wdth=100. Noto Sans SemiBold uses the same coordinates
from the existing Noto Sans variable font. Original font licenses accompany the
TTFs. sources:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifdisplay
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans
tools/build-social-font-metrics.py generates the shared browser/Worker advance
widths from these exact TTFs. It requires fontTools at authoring time only.

Brand marks:
The compact portrait and horizontal marks use the original outlined symbol and
wordmark from brand/fourwalls_logo_vertical.svg. The small REAL ESTATE tagline
is omitted for social use. These logos are vector-derived, never AI-generated.
